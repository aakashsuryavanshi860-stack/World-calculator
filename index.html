    Privacy Policy

    Last Updated: 2026

    At World Calculators, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by World Calculators and how we use it.

    1. Data Processing in Browser

    Most of our calculators (Basic, Scientific, Financial, etc.) run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We do not store or transmit your calculator inputs to any server.

    2. File Uploads (PDF & Image Tools)

    When you use our PDF or Image tools, the files are processed locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers. If a specific tool requires server-side processing, files are deleted automatically immediately after processing.

    3. Cookies and Web Beacons

    Like any other website, World Calculators uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

    4. Google AdSense & Analytics

    We may use Google AdSense and Google Analytics. Google uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt-out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

    5. Consent

    By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
